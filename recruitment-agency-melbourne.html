Critical infrastructure and data centre recruitment in Melbourne

Melbourne's data centre market is growing fast, with committed projects set to almost triple live capacity. Big campuses are moving from planning into construction and commissioning, which means sustained demand for electricians, commissioning teams and, soon after, the operations staff who run them.

ZEST recruits for Melbourne employers across that whole lifecycle, and helps Victorian tradespeople find their way into it.

Where the work is in Victoria

  • Port Melbourne: home to NEXTDC's roughly $2 billion M4 campus, fast-tracked by the Victorian Government.
  • Melbourne's north-west and west: AirTrunk's MEL2 adds 354MW+, lifting its Melbourne investment past $7 billion, with total MEL1 and MEL2 capacity above 630MW by 2027.
  • Truganina, Mickleham and Donnybrook: emerging as Melbourne's own hyperscale corridor.

Frequently asked questions

Does ZEST recruit in Melbourne?

Yes. We recruit electricians, technicians, project coordinators and facilities staff for data centre, energy and critical infrastructure employers in Melbourne and across Victoria.

Do you supply contract electricians in Melbourne?

Yes. We supply contract, temporary and permanent electricians and technicians in Melbourne, all holding current VIC electrical licences and the site tickets your project requires.

Do your Melbourne electricians hold Victorian licences?

Yes. Every electrician we put forward for Victorian work holds a current Energy Safe Victoria licence, along with the site tickets and cabling registration the role needs.
