1. About this policy

Zest Hire, trading as ZEST ("we", "us", "our"), is a recruitment agency placing people into critical infrastructure, data centre, energy and construction roles across Australia. This policy explains how we handle personal information about candidates, employer clients and anyone else who deals with us, including through zesthire.com.au.

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

2. What we collect

If you're a candidate, we may collect:

  • your name, email, phone number and location
  • your CV, work history, qualifications, skills and the roles you're interested in
  • licences, tickets, certifications and their expiry dates (for example electrical licences, high-voltage, working at heights or site inductions)
  • your work rights, availability, notice period, salary or rate expectations and roster preferences
  • references and the results of checks you agree to, such as reference, police or fit-for-work checks
  • notes from our conversations with you, and feedback from employers about your applications or placements

If you're an employer or work for one, we may collect your name, job title, company, contact details, and details of the roles and projects you're hiring for.

We only collect sensitive information (such as health information relevant to a fit-for-work check, or a criminal history check) where it's reasonably needed for a role and you have consented, or where the law allows it.

3. How we collect it

Wherever we can, we collect information directly from you: when you fill in a form on our website, apply for a job, upload your CV, email or call us, or meet with us. We may also collect information from referees you nominate, employers we place you with, background check providers (with your consent), and public professional sources such as LinkedIn or job boards where you've made your profile available.

You don't have to give us your information, but if you don't, we may not be able to represent you for roles or respond to your enquiry.

4. Why we use it

  • to assess your suitability for roles and put you forward for them, with your agreement
  • to match candidates with employers and manage placements, contracts and onboarding
  • to run reference and other checks you've agreed to, and keep your licences and tickets up to date
  • to respond to enquiries and requests for shortlists
  • to send you job alerts and updates you've asked for (see section 8)
  • to run our business, keep records and meet our legal obligations

We use software tools, including AI-assisted tools, to help read CVs and organise candidate records. People at ZEST make the decisions about who to put forward for a role.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only where needed for the purposes above, including with:

  • prospective employers: we'll only send your CV or details to an employer for a specific role with your agreement
  • your referees and check providers you've consented to
  • service providers who help us run our business, such as our email, cloud storage, database, website hosting and recruitment software providers. They may only use the information to provide their services to us
  • government agencies or others where the law requires or allows it

6. Overseas storage

Our website form submissions and CV uploads are stored in Australia (Sydney). Some of our service providers store or process information outside Australia, including email delivery (Japan and the United States), cloud storage and document tools (the United States and other countries where Google operates), website hosting and website analytics (the United States). We take reasonable steps to use reputable providers with strong security and privacy practices.

7. Storage & security

We store personal information electronically with access limited to ZEST staff who need it. CVs uploaded through our website go to a private storage area that can't be viewed publicly. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and the security controls of our providers to protect information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law, and then delete or de-identify it. If you'd like us to remove your details, just ask (see section 12).

If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we'll notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as required under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

8. Job alerts & marketing

If you join our talent network, we'll contact you about roles that match your skills. You can opt out at any time by replying "unsubscribe", using the unsubscribe link in our emails, or contacting us.

9. Access & correction

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it's wrong, out of date or incomplete. Contact us using the details below. We'll respond within 30 days. There's no charge to make a request. If we refuse access in the limited circumstances the law allows, we'll explain why.

10. Our website

We use Google Analytics (through Google Tag Manager) to understand how people use our website, such as which pages are visited, how long people stay, the type of device and browser used, and their approximate location. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. We don't use this information to identify you, and we don't send your form details (like your name, email or CV) to Google Analytics. We don't use advertising cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics using Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Like most websites, the services that host our site and supply its fonts and images may also record technical information such as your IP address and browser type. When you submit a form, we receive the details you enter, plus the page you submitted it from.

11. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we've handled your personal information, please contact us first and we'll do our best to resolve it within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
