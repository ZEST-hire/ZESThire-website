Pay guide

Data centre electrician salary in Australia.

A snapshot of what data centre electricians and technicians are being offered in 2026, and what pushes pay up or down.

Join the talent network →Browse live jobs

Data centre work is some of the better-paid electrical work in Australia, especially on shift rosters and in tight markets like Western Sydney. Pay varies a lot by role type, roster, state and experience, so here's a snapshot of what's been advertised recently, plus what drives the differences.

Snapshot of advertised pay (late 2026)

Figures are drawn from roles publicly advertised on Australian job boards and recruiter sites in October to November 2026. They're a guide only, not an offer, and individual packages vary.

What pushes data centre pay up

  • Shift rosters. 24/7 operations roles usually carry shift loadings, which is why shift electricians often out-earn day-work peers.
  • Live critical-environment experience. Time on UPS, generators, switchgear and BMS in a live facility is valued highly.
  • Commissioning skills. Electricians who can support testing and handover are in short supply.
  • Extra registrations. ACMA cabling registration with fibre and structured endorsements broadens the roles you can take.
  • Location. Western Sydney and Melbourne's west are competitive markets right now, and regional or FIFO work often includes allowances.

Contract vs permanent

Contract and labour-hire roles on construction projects usually pay an hourly rate with overtime, and can be very lucrative during busy phases. Permanent operations roles tend to quote an annual salary plus super, with more stability, training and progression into supervisor, technician or facilities roles.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a data centre electrician earn in Australia?

Based on roles advertised in late 2026, shift electricians in live data centres in Western Sydney were being offered around $145,000 to $146,000 plus super, while contract construction and fit-out roles were commonly advertised at around $50 to $65 per hour plus overtime or allowances.

Do data centre electricians earn more than other electricians?

Often, yes, especially on 24/7 shift rosters, which usually carry shift loadings, and for electricians with live critical-environment or commissioning experience.

Is contract or permanent data centre work better paid?

Contract construction roles often pay higher hourly rates with overtime during busy phases. Permanent operations roles offer an annual salary plus super with more stability and progression.
