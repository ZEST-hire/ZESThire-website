Electricians are the backbone of what we do

Every critical site, from a hyperscale data centre to a substation upgrade, needs licensed electricians before it needs anything else. They're also the hardest people to find right now. Data centres, renewables, transmission, defence and the Brisbane 2032 build are all chasing the same pool of tradespeople.

ZEST is an electrical recruitment agency that focuses on that pool. We know the difference between a construction sparky who loves a fast fit-out and a shift electrician who wants a stable 4-on-4-off roster in a live facility, and we match people accordingly.

Types of electricians we place

  • Construction electricians for data centre halls, commercial builds and infrastructure projects: cable tray, cable pulling, terminations, switchboards and LV distribution.
  • Shift and maintenance electricians for live data centres and critical facilities, working with UPS, generators, ATS, PDUs and BMS on rotating rosters.
  • Commissioning electricians and technicians supporting integrated systems testing, pre-functional checks and handover.
  • Electrical supervisors and leading hands running crews on site.
  • Electrical trade assistants and apprentices to round out a crew.
  • Electrical technicians, including those with high-voltage, hazardous areas or instrumentation experience.

What we check before you see a CV

Licensing is state-based in Australia, and data centre and infrastructure clients are rightly strict about compliance. Before we put an electrician forward we confirm:

  • A current electrical licence for the state the work is in
  • Relevant tickets: white card, working at heights, EWP, confined space, first aid and CPR/LVR as required
  • ACMA cabling registration and endorsements where the role involves data or fibre
  • Right to work in Australia, and police checks or site-specific clearances where the client needs them
  • Availability, roster preference and willingness to travel, relocate or FIFO

Contract, temp or permanent

Most of our electrical clients use us for a mix. Contract crews to get a hall energised on time, temp cover for shutdowns and leave, and permanent hires for operations teams who need people to stay. Tell us what the project looks like and we'll tell you honestly what's achievable in the timeframe.

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of electricians do you recruit?

Construction, shift, maintenance and commissioning electricians, electrical supervisors, leading hands, electrical technicians and trade assistants, mainly for data centres, energy and critical infrastructure projects.

Do your electricians hold current state licences?

Yes. We confirm every electrician holds a current electrical licence for the state the work is in, plus the tickets, cabling registration and clearances the role requires, before we put them forward.

How quickly can you supply electricians?

It depends on the location, roster and tickets required. Tell us the project and start date and we'll give you an honest view of what's achievable and when.

Do you recruit electricians for FIFO and regional work?

Yes. We recruit for metro, regional and FIFO/DIDO work across WA, QLD, NSW and VIC, and we track which candidates are open to travel or relocating.
