A data centre recruiter that speaks the language of the site

Australia's data centre build-out is one of the biggest construction and operations programs the country has seen, and it runs on skilled hands. Every new hall needs electricians pulling cable and terminating switchboards, technicians commissioning UPS and generators, HVAC techs keeping precision cooling stable, and coordinators holding the program together.

ZEST is a specialist data centre recruitment agency focused on exactly those people. We don't try to be everything to everyone. We recruit the trades, technicians and project support roles that build, commission and run critical facilities, and we do it across four states.

Why data centre hiring is different

Data centre work looks like commercial electrical on paper, but the expectations on site are higher. Uptime is everything, method statements and permits are strict, and a lot of the work happens in or next to live, energised environments. Many operations roles are 24/7 shift rosters, and construction projects move fast once a hall is released.

That means the usual approach of posting an ad and seeing who applies tends to fall short. The best people are usually already working, often on another precinct, and they move through referrals and trusted recruiters. We spend our time in those networks so you don't have to.

Frequently asked questions

Which data centre roles does ZEST recruit for?

We recruit licensed electricians, electrical supervisors, data centre technicians, HVAC and critical-cooling technicians, structured cablers, commissioning coordinators, project coordinators, document controllers and facilities managers for data centre construction, fit-out and live operations.

Which locations do you cover?

We recruit across Western Australia, Queensland, New South Wales and Victoria, including Perth, Brisbane, Sydney (Macquarie Park and Western Sydney) and Melbourne, plus regional and FIFO projects.

Do you supply contractors or permanent staff?

Both. We place contract and temporary crews for build and commissioning phases, and permanent staff for operations teams.

Can you find electricians who haven't worked in a data centre before?

Yes. Plenty of strong commercial, industrial, mining and telecommunications electricians move into data centres successfully. We screen for the experience and attitude that translates, and we're upfront with you about who has direct data centre experience and who doesn't.
